Jar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Jar is a clipboard history app for macOS.

  • Everything you copy is stored only on your Mac, inside Jar's own sandboxed container.
  • Jar has no network access of any kind. It cannot send data anywhere. There are no analytics, no accounts, and no crash reporting beyond what macOS itself provides.
  • Jar ignores content marked as confidential or transient by other apps (password managers in particular), and lets you exclude any app from capture.
  • You can delete individual items, clear the history, or remove all of Jar's data with Settings → History → “Clear everything, including pins…” (macOS keeps an app's container folder when the app is moved to the Trash; delete it to remove every trace).
  • The optional “direct paste” permission (shown by macOS under Accessibility) is used only to press ⌘V on your behalf after you choose an item. Separately, where macOS allows it, Jar reads only the on-screen position of the text cursor in the frontmost app, to open its window next to it; Jar never reads the text or any other content of other apps.
